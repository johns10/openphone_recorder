# How to Manage Contacts in Discussit 

This guide will walk you through the steps to manage contacts in Discussit. Follow the instructions below to create, update, and delete contacts. 

1. Open Discussit: Visit [app.discussit.ai](app.discussit.ai) in your web browser and log in to your account.
2. Access the [Contacts Menu](app.discussit.ai/contacts): Once you are logged in, click on the "Contacts" menu located on the left-hand side of the screen.
3. View Existing Contacts: In the Contacts menu, you will see a list of existing contacts displayed on the left-hand side of the screen.
4. Create a [New Contact](app.discussit.ai/contacts/new): To create a new contact, click on the green plus icon located in the top menu. This will redirect you to the "New Contac" page.
5. Fill in Contact Details: On the "New Contact" page, enter the contact's first and last name, as well as the company they work for. Additionally, you can add their relationship to the company.
6.  Add Phone Number: If you want to add a phone number for the contact, click the green plus button under the "Phone Numbers" section in the form. You can even add international phone numbers.
7.  Save Contact: Once you have entered all the necessary information, click the "Save Contact" button to create the new contact.
8.  Update an Existing Contact: To update an existing contact, select the contact from the menu on the left-hand side. You can then make any necessary changes to the contact's details.
9.  Delete a Contact: If you wish to delete a contact, locate the contact in the menu on the left-hand side and click on the trash icon. Confirm the deletion when prompted. 

That's it! You have successfully learned how to manage contacts in Discussit. Feel free to explore other features and functionalities of the application.
