# Discussit Executive Summary

At Discussit, we are building a general purpose conversation intelligence platform that helps our customers get maximum value out of their work conversations.

We've built a cloud-baased SaaS platform that ingests conversation data from multiple sources like Zoom, Gmail, and Openphone, and uses AI to provide our customers with summarization, analysis, and reporting tools that represent the voice of the customer at scale.

Our application will be used to reduce sales cycles, reduce preparation time before calls, have better access to tactical information, find lost deals, develop the right products, extract tribal knowledge, and more.

We launched our MVP in May 2023, and were able to gain both traction and revenue. We earned several sales, which led us to develop the first version of our product, which went to production in late August 2023. We've launched with a usage-based pricing to ensure early profitability.

We are currently performing preliminary sales calls to determine product market fit, onboard early users, and understand which features are required to establish a consistent revenue pipeline as quickly as possible.

## Competitive Analysis + Market Size

An obvious market to apply this technology is Sales Enablement (valued at USD 18B in 2021). The opportunity in this space is best demonstrated by Gong (est. 2015), whose revenue run rate hit $178M in 2023, and is currently valued at $7.3B.

Unsurprisingly, there has been a number of entrants to this market since LLM's became widely consumed by other software companies including Fathom, Avoma, Chorus, and Clari copilot. Many of these companies have similar value propositions, largely focused on recording, transcription, and automatic note taking.

Should we find the Sales Enablement market to crowded, or competitive, we will pivot to use cases suited for the Customer Success Software market (valued at USD 1.2B in 2021, projected to grow to 7B by 2030), which is less crowded.

We have additional fallback positions in other markets as well, which we will leverage to earn early growth, and continue to grow in the future.

## Implementation Strategy

There are two risks that must be mitigated to achieve our first consistent sales, and early stage growth: being unable to dislodge our more established competitors, and being outpaced by upcoming LLM-powered entrants.

We plan to mitigate these risks by executing our go-to-market in phases. In the first, we'll focus on identifying an easily scalable segment to get early sales wins. In the second, we'll focus on growing in that niche. 

### Phase 1: Establish Product Market Fit

We plan to use traditional sales tactics with our version 1 product to get one on one interactions with customers. We will try to close all customers, and drill down to technical objections to establish a must-have roadmap that closes consistently. To execute this phase, we need to hire our founding team. Our estimated run rate in this phase is $200k/year (living expenses for 2 + minimal tooling and resources).

### Phase 2: Early Stage Growth

We plan to use contractors to help us implement automation, self-service tools, and conversation AI entities to manage interactions, and automate our operations as much as possible. We plan to hire representatives for 5 key areas who will guide, manage, and maintain implementations and contractors. Our estimated run rate in this phase is $700k/year (7 salaries).
