# How to Import Meetings into Discussit 

This guide will walk you through the process of importing meetings from Zoom into Discussit. 

Follow the steps below to complete the task: 

1. Open the Discussit web app by visiting [app.discussit.ai](https://app.discussit.ai).
2. Navigate to the "[Meetings](https://app.discussit.ai/meetings)" menu in Discussit.
3. Click on the green plus sign icon to add a new meeting.
4. In the file explorer window that appears, locate and select the Zoom folder where your meetings are stored.
5. Click on the "Select" button to choose the folder.
6. Once the folder is selected, click on the "View Files" button to allow Discussit permission.
7. Discussit will now start importing your Zoom meetings one by one. It will not import the next meeting until the current one finishes importing.
8. To transcribe a meeting, click on the microphone button next to the meeting listed in the menu.
9. The microphone button will turn into a spinner, indicating that the meeting is being transcribed.
10. Once the meeting has been transcribed, the microphone button will turn into a green checkbox.
11. After importing a meeting, you need to assign participants to the conversation.
12. Click the meeting you want to work on.
13. Look for the "Participants" section.
14. Click on down caret icon.
15. A dropdown menu will appear. If you don't see the desired contact in the dropdown, you can start typing their name to search for them.
16. Select all the contacts that participated in the meeting.
17. Once you have selected all the contacts, a "Create Conversation" button will appear.
18. Click on the "Create Conversation" button.
19. Click on the conversation name to go to the actual conversation page where you can start summarizing the content of the meeting. 

By following these steps, you will be able to successfully import meetings from Zoom into Discussit, assign participants, and get ready to summarize the conversations.
